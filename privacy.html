Privacy policy - HSQ Operations

Last updated: 24 November 2026.

HSQ Operations is a private application used only by the owner of the Heavy Side Quest accounts. It is not offered to other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, it uploads Heavy Side Quest's own videos to its own YouTube channel and Instagram account, and reads the statistics of those videos (views, likes, comments, followers). It reads public information about public creator accounts (profile name, follower count, public posts) for market research.

YouTube API Services

HSQ Operations uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

What it does not do

It does not collect personal data from other people, does not sell or share any data, and does not use data for advertising. Google user data is used only to provide the features above and is never transferred to third parties.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens and statistics are stored on the owner's private server, encrypted, and are not shared. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account settings (Security, Third-party access) or from Meta's business settings; the stored data can be deleted on request.

Contact

Through the Heavy Side Quest Instagram account: @heavysidequest.
